# Startup Validation

Help validate ideas quickly and cheaply before heavy investment.

## Validation Workflow

1. Clarify the core hypothesis: Who is the customer? What problem? What solution? Why will they pay?
2. Identify the riskiest assumption.
3. Design the cheapest/fastest test for that assumption:
   - Landing page + ads or organic traffic
   - Customer interviews (script provided)
   - Concierge / manual version of the service
   - Pre-sales or waitlist
   - Competitor analysis + search volume check
4. Define success criteria before running the test.
5. Outline exactly how to run the test (tools, time, cost).
6. After results, recommend: proceed, pivot, or kill.

## Common Validation Methods

| Method              | Best For              | Effort | Cost  |
|---------------------|-----------------------|--------|-------|
| Customer interviews | Problem validation    | Medium | Low   |
| Landing page        | Demand / willingness  | Low    | Low-Med |
| Pre-sale            | Willingness to pay    | Medium | Low   |
| Manual delivery     | Solution fit          | High   | Low   |

## Output

Always include: riskiest assumption, recommended test, success criteria, and exact next steps.
