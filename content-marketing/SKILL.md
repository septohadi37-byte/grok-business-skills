# Content Marketing

Create focused content strategies that support business goals.

## Workflow

1. Clarify goal: brand awareness, lead generation, sales, SEO, or community building.
2. Identify target audience and their content preferences / platforms.
3. Define content pillars (3-5 themes that align with the business).
4. Generate specific content ideas under each pillar (titles, formats, angles).
5. Recommend formats and platforms (short video, carousel, blog, thread, email, etc.).
6. Build a simple 2-4 week content calendar.
7. Include one clear CTA pattern for the content.

## Content Pillar Examples

- Educational (how-to, tips, mistakes)
- Behind the scenes / founder story
- Customer results / social proof
- Industry insights / trends
- Product or offer focused

## Output Format

- Goal & audience
- Content pillars
- Idea list with suggested format
- Sample content calendar
