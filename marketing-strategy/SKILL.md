# Marketing Strategy

Build actionable marketing strategies tailored to the business stage and resources.

## Workflow

1. Understand the offering: what is being sold, to whom, current stage (idea / early / growth), and available budget & time.
2. Define the target customer clearly (demographics, psychographics, where they hang out).
3. Choose 2-4 primary channels based on where the customer is and the budget. Prefer channels with fast feedback loops early on.
4. For each channel, outline:
   - Specific tactics
   - Content or offer needed
   - Expected effort
   - Success metric
5. Create a 30-day action plan with weekly priorities.
6. Identify the one "primary metric" to track.
7. Suggest simple ways to test and iterate.

## Channel Selection Guide

| Stage       | Budget   | Preferred Channels                  |
|-------------|----------|-------------------------------------|
| Idea/Early  | Low      | Content, communities, cold outreach, partnerships |
| Early       | Medium   | Paid ads (small), SEO, email, content |
| Growth      | Higher   | Paid, SEO, affiliates, PR           |

## Output Format

- Target customer summary
- Recommended channels with rationale
- 30-day action plan
- Key metrics to track
